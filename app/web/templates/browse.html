Questions guests ask

Is the payment real?

No. Every booking on this site is a TEST booking and no real money moves. The checkout page also lets you try a failed payment.

Can I cancel?

Yes, from your booking page before the start time. Cancel 48 hours or more before and you get the full amount back. Later than that, you get 80% back and your host keeps 20%. The cancel page shows the exact amount before you confirm.

When do I get the address?

Once your host confirms. Your booking page then shows the map pin and a landmark near the door, and you get the same details by email.

What if my host cannot take my booking?

If your host says no, or does not answer in time, the booking is cancelled and you get the full amount back automatically.

I do not speak Hindi. Is that a problem?

No. Your booking page has a text translation helper: type what you want to say and show your host the Hindi.

Who are the hosts?

Local families who share their food, craft, music or culture at home. During this demo, the hosts are played by our team and labelled role-play.

How many people can come?

Each experience shows its guest limit, and each date shows how many seats are left.
