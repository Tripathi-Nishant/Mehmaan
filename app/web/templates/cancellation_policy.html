If you cancel

When you cancelYou get back
48 hours or more before the start100%
Less than 48 hours before the start80%, and your host keeps 20%

Example: a booking of ₹3,000 cancelled the evening before gives you ₹2,400 back, and your host keeps ₹600 for the time and food they prepared. If an amount does not divide evenly, the extra paisa goes to you.

If your host cannot host you

If your host says no, or does not reply within {{ reply_hours }} hours, your booking is cancelled and you get the full amount back automatically.

How to cancel

Open your booking page from the link in your email and choose "Cancel booking". The next screen shows exactly how much you get back before you confirm, and cancelling twice never refunds twice.
