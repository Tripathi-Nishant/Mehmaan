From guests

  • Your name and email, to hold your booking and send you booking emails.
  • Number of guests, so your host can prepare.
  • Dietary needs or allergies, only if you choose to tell us. We pass them to your host with your request.

There are no accounts or passwords. Your booking page is reached through a private link in your email; anyone with that link can see the booking, so keep it to yourself.

From hosts

  • Their Telegram account, what they tell us about their experience, and the listing we create from it.
  • A map pin and a gate photo. The pin is shown only to guests whose booking the host confirmed. The gate photo is never shown publicly.

Who processes it

  • Telegram carries messages between Mehmaan and hosts.
  • Gnani converts host messages between speech and text.
  • An AI language model helps turn what hosts say into English listing text.
  • An email provider delivers your booking emails.

We do not sell information or use tracking or advertising cookies.

This is a demo built for a challenge. Payments are TEST payments, no card details are collected, and no real money moves.
