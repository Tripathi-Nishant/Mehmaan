This is a demo

Mehmaan is a demo built for a challenge. Bookings are TEST bookings, payments are simulated, and no real money moves. Hosts shown during the demo are played by our team and are labelled "Demo host (role-play)".

Booking

  • Prices are per person and fixed. The total is shown before you pay.
  • Your seats are held for {{ hold_minutes }} minutes while you pay. After that they are released for other guests.
  • A booking is confirmed only when your host says yes. If they do not reply within {{ reply_hours }} hours, you get a full refund automatically.
  • The exact location is shared only after confirmation.

Cancelling

Cancellations and refunds follow our cancellation policy, the same for every home.

Being a good guest

You are visiting a family's home. Arrive on time, follow the house's customs, and ask before taking photos of people.
